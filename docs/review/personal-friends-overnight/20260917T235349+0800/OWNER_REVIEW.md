Use the private synthetic A/B handoff and the isolated review API. These checks are planned for roughly 30–45 minutes, not guaranteed. Stop only the failing branch and retain its draft/context.

1. **Candidate identity and presentation.** Confirm the phone is on compatible app/runtime 0.2.6, then confirm O60 after the owner manually publishes/downloads it. Review representative Day/Sunset/Night screens, native Mapbox, safe areas, keyboard, and larger text.
2. **Personal Cairn journey.** Run a short real indoor/outdoor Hike or Run; create an empty Quick Cairn and a named full Cairn; verify `Cairn saved`, continued recording, Finish, Activity Detail, Memory, and All Cairns. This is personal GPS evidence, not a Friends Encounter test.
3. **Route journey.** Make a supported geometry correction, save/reopen through Trails, and use the Route from Hike and Run pre-start. Separately open the prepared friend Route read-only and confirm it is a temporary reference, not an owned copy.
4. **Friends roles and Memory.** With prepared A/B, verify author sharing, Personal/Combined/single-friend scope, overlap/source labels, Friends-visible Cairn/Route read-only behavior, revoke/remove/block calm states, and no per-person permission request workflow.
5. **Offline/relaunch/reconnect.** While a personal Activity and borrowed Route reference are active, go offline, relaunch/recover, finish or discard, reconnect, and verify the borrowed geometry existed only for that outing. Then verify known revoke/expiry removes friend content calmly.

Do not spoof production presence or treat the seeded Encounter as physical proof. A physical friend-only Encounter and NZ field work remain separate later exercises.
