# Controlled Public Cairn pilot contract

Public v1 applies to text Cairns only. `Only me`, `Friends`, and `Public` stay
one simple author choice in a pilot-enabled realm. Quick Cairn remains private
by default; an empty Cairn remains valid personal content but is not eligible
for stranger discovery. `A moment here` is display fallback, never authored
text.

Publication states are `not_public`, `pending`, `published`, `rejected`,
`suspended`, and `withdrawn`. Approval binds the exact Cairn content revision
and publication episode. A material edit or change away from Public removes
the old revision from distribution. Returning to Public creates a new pending
episode. Delete dominates delayed create/edit/approval/read responses.

Discovery requires a published revision at original qualified observation
time, supported finalized real-Activity presence, current access, and no hide,
block, suspension, withdrawal, or deletion. Simulator/unknown evidence, map
pan, friend Fog, planned/borrowed Route, Final smoothing, or arbitrary ID is
never qualification. A previously encountered, still-authorized object may be
reread without another physical visit.

Scene selection is centralized and deterministic: hard filters, local place
relevance, hidden/repeat/duplicate suppression, author diversity, stable tie
break. A scene contains at most three entries, at most one per author, and
presents at most one newly surfaced card. Zero is valid. Retrieval is bounded
and paginated; no global exact-coordinate/text catalog exists.

Thanks is authenticated, idempotent, one effective acknowledgement per
viewer/resource, unavailable to self, and discloses no viewer location.
Hide is immediate locally and durable with retry. Block is enforced by APIs,
caches, legacy reads, and discovery. Report is persisted, retryable,
rate/duplicate protected, contains minimal context, and is not an emergency
service or automatic permanent punishment.

Operators are server-authorized. They inspect and decide an exact revision;
stale decisions fail. Credentials and roles never ship in the normal app.
Production Public endpoints remain server-disabled; only the isolated review
realm may enable the pilot.
