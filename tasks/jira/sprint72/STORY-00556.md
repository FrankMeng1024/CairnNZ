# STORY-00556: 登录页数据在本机提示 + iOS 低电量警告

**Epic**: E-UX · **Sprint**: 72 · **Points**: 2 · **Owner**: Frontend + UX · **Status**: Done

## Description
Two independent UX safety nets:
1. AuthScreen shows a reassuring line "Your history is saved on this device" so a returning user doesn't panic thinking their tracks got wiped when the 30-day token finally expires.
2. When tracking active + iOS Low Power Mode is on → one-time alert warning the user their background recording may be throttled by iOS.

## 改动 A — AuthScreen 提示
`app/src/screens/AuthScreen.tsx` 底部/顶部小字（14pt secondary color）:
> Your tracks stay on this device. Sign in to sync new activity to the cloud.

## 改动 B — Low Power Mode 警告
1. 引入 `expo-battery.getPowerStateAsync()` 
2. `useTrackingStore.startTracking()` 成功后调用一次 + AppState=active 时也调用
3. 如果 `lowPowerMode === true` 且当前 tracking:
   - 检查 AsyncStorage 里 `cairn_lpm_warned_ts`（一次性 flag，24小时内不重复）
   - 弹 Alert "iOS Low Power Mode is on — this may limit background GPS. Consider turning it off or keeping the app in foreground."
   - 更新 flag ts
