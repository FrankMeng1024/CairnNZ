# STORY-00058: AuthScreen Splash Visual Balance

**Epic**: E-006 UI/UX
**Sprint**: Sprint 22
**Sprint Goal**: Design token hygiene, stat display polish, and icon system consistency
**Points**: 3
**Owner**: Frontend Dev
**Status**: Done

## Description
As a first-time user opening the app, I want the authentication splash screen to feel premium with visual balance — the logo area should be substantial, centered with a background that grounds it, and the tagline should feel like a brand statement rather than floating text.

## Expected Result
AuthScreen splash has: the cairn logo centered in a visually grounded area with a subtle background gradient or panel, the tagline styled with appropriate weight and spacing, and the CTA buttons anchored firmly at the bottom with adequate breathing room. The overall composition feels like a premium outdoor app (comparable to AllTrails, Strava onboarding).

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Logo area occupies at least 40% of the screen height — logo, brand name, and tagline all vertically centered in this region
- [ ] A subtle background treatment behind the logo area: either a soft radial gradient (from `Colors.primaryLight` at center to transparent) or a light topo-line pattern, giving depth without overwhelming
- [ ] Tagline `"Leave a mark. Guide the next."` rendered in two lines with line break after "mark." — each line has appropriate weight (regular or medium weight, NOT bold)
- [ ] CTA buttons ("Create Account" / "Sign In") have at least 56px height — full-width pill-shaped with generous internal padding
- [ ] Button area clearly separated from logo area with enough whitespace (>= 32px gap)
- [ ] Entrance animation: logo + tagline fade-in (opacity 0→1) over 400ms with a subtle 8px upward translate — creates "rising from the trail" feel
- [ ] No console errors at 390px viewport

## Dependencies
- Depends on: none
- Blocks: none

## Notes
AuthScreen was redesigned in Sprint 21 (STORY-00051) but visual balance between logo area and CTA zone still feels compressed. AllTrails and Strava both have large, bold splash compositions. Competitor reference: AllTrails splash has bold hero image + minimal text. We can't use images but can use the gradient + composition to achieve comparable premium feel.
