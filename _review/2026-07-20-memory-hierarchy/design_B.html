Design B — 3 Decisions & Reasoning

D1 · Popover above icon (A) — 240×auto pt, spring 220ms scale+fade from icon anchor. Closes on tap-outside or re-tap icon. Rationale: it never leaves the corner, doesn't cover markers on the right 2/3 of the map, and its arrow keeps the mental link to the trigger.

D2 · Left dot + light meta (B) — green dot = explored, hollow dot = unexplored. Short "23 marks" suffix only. No neon, no chips, no checkmarks. Handbook-quiet.

D3 · Single-tap-up + siblings (variant of B, no breadcrumb) — one "↑ Shanghai" row acts as breadcrumb + up button; below it, exactly the current-level siblings. Max 5 rows enforced by scrolling the sibling list. Zero decorative UI.
