Design B v2 — Three Decisions

Icon (stacked layers): three offset trapezoids read as "zoom out to a wider layer" — orthogonal to the right-side Target crosshair (which points inward at "me"). Left = zoom out, right = zoom in.

Panel (single-level siblings): shows only the current level's peers under a breadcrumb. Current district highlighted. Max 5 rows keeps it thumb-reachable and non-scrolling for most cities.

No-mark expression: filled sepia dot = has marks + count; hollow sepia ring + italic "explored" = walked-through only; dashed grey ring + "locked" = never visited. Three states, no color overload.
