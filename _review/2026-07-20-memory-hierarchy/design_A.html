D1 · Popover above icon (A). 232×~200pt, spring 220ms, tap-outside / tap-icon to close. Small footprint honors "80% won't use"; visually tied to trigger (arrow), never covers fog/markers or center of map.

D3 · Only-explored + "N more" hint (C). Panel stays under 5 rows always. "You are here" pinned on top; up-arrow returns to parent level. Discovery of new regions is a deliberate act, not a scan of 16 grey items.
