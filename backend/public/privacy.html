Cairn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026 · Cairn hiking companion app

This policy explains what data Cairn collects, how we use it, and the rights you have. It applies to the Cairn mobile app and the accounts you create in it. If anything below is unclear, email privacy@cairnapp.nz.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: name and email you provide when you sign up, plus a hashed password. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the same information from those providers.
  • Location data: GPS coordinates recorded while you are actively tracking a hike or run. We do not collect background location outside of an active session unless you explicitly enable it.
  • Content you create: hikes, cairns (map markers), notes, and photos you attach to markers.
  • Device diagnostics: crash logs and error breadcrumbs, tied to your account, only when you have opted in via Settings.
  • Feedback and support: whatever you send via the in-app "Send feedback" form.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the core features of the app — showing your hikes, syncing across devices, letting you plant and rediscover cairns.
  • To share your hikes and cairns with people you have explicitly added as friends. Nothing you record is shared publicly by default.
  • To improve the app: aggregated, anonymised counts of feature usage and crash traces.
  • To reply to you when you contact us.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your location or hikes for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except (a) the providers that run our servers (Alibaba Cloud) and analytics, (b) if legally required, or (c) with your explicit consent.

4. Where your data lives

Cairn's servers run in Alibaba Cloud (Shanghai region). Your data is stored in a MySQL database and object storage bucket in the same region. Transport is over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage.

5. Your rights

  • View: everything Cairn holds about you is visible in the app.
  • Correct: edit your name, email, and password in Settings.
  • Reset: use "Reset my map memory" in Settings to clear location history.
  • Delete: use "Delete account" in Settings. This opens an email to privacy@cairnapp.nz that we will action within 5 business days.
  • Export: email us to request a copy of your data.

6. Children

Cairn is not intended for children under 13. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be surfaced in the app.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@cairnapp.nz
General support: support@cairnapp.nz
