Cairn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026 · Cairn hiking companion app

This policy explains what data Cairn collects, how we use it, and the rights you have. It applies to the Cairn mobile app and the accounts you create in it. If anything below is unclear, email privacy@cairnapp.nz.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: the name and email associated with your account, your sign-in provider, and a hashed password when you use Cairn email sign-in. Cairn never stores your plain-text password.
  • Location and Activity data: GPS observations, routes, distance, duration and related records while you record a Hike or Run. With your separate permission, Cairn can also use foreground location to update exploration while the app is open and no Activity is recording.
  • Content you create: Activities, Routes, Cairns (map markers), notes, marker media, Memory points and derived explored regions.
  • Reliability diagnostics: limited app, device, crash and event information used to diagnose failures. Diagnostic uploads remove credential-shaped values and precise coordinates from real-location events before sending. Separately gated internal QA tools may upload labelled synthetic test evidence.
  • Feedback and support: the message, category, app version and delivery identifier you send through Cairn feedback or support.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the core features of the app — showing your hikes, syncing across devices, letting you plant and rediscover cairns.
  • To apply the visibility choices attached to Cairns, Activities and Routes and to support friend features.
  • To diagnose crashes, failed sync, Activity recording defects and other reliability problems.
  • To reply to you when you contact us.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your location or hikes for advertising.
  • We do not share your data except with service providers needed to operate Cairn, when required by law, or when you direct Cairn to share it.

4. Where your data lives

Cairn's servers run in Alibaba Cloud's Shanghai region. Account and product data is stored in Cairn's database and object storage. Transport to Cairn services uses HTTPS and passwords owned by Cairn are hashed with bcrypt. Where Cairn offers road-aware route reconstruction, the relevant route coordinates may be sent directly to Mapbox to request candidate map geometry; Cairn remains responsible for deciding whether that geometry represents the journey.

5. Your rights

  • Access and export: request a JSON copy from Settings, follow its preparation status there, and use the download link when it is ready. Ready links expire after 24 hours.
  • Correct: edit your display name and, for Cairn password accounts, change your password in Settings. Account email is not currently changeable in the app; contact support if it is wrong.
  • Delete exploration history: remove Memory points and derived explored regions while keeping Activities, Routes and Cairns.
  • Delete account: request deletion in Settings. Cairn disables the account and its existing sessions immediately. You can restore server-backed data by signing in during the next seven days. After that, Cairn permanently deletes the account and account-owned product data, exports, feedback and account-linked diagnostics. Account data on the requesting device is cleared when the request succeeds; unsynced device-only data cannot be restored.

6. Children

Cairn is not intended for children under 13. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be surfaced in the app.

8. Contact
